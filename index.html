Sleep basics that actually help

      
    
        
  • Keep bedtime within about an hour of the same time every night, weekends included.
    • 
        
  • Put your phone away 30–60 minutes before bed, or at least across the room.
    • 
        
  • Skip caffeine (soda, energy drinks, coffee) after about 3 PM.
    • 
        
  • Get some daylight in the morning. It helps set your body clock.
    • 
        
  • If you can't sleep after 20 minutes, get up and do something calm, then try again.
